## 4.1 First-`2%` Token Centerpiece Annotation & Minimal-Fluff Tool Front Ends
*(Directional / single-source observation from `audiototext.com` & Content Warehouse API Leak commentary; live Google annotation weights are unobservable.)*
1. **Place the Primary Tool in the First `2%` of Tokens:** On single-page utility microsites (`audiototext.com`: `~50 → ~8,000` clicks/day), place the giant primary interactive control (`upload button`, `calculator`, `tool canvas`) + clear friction-removal cue (`"no sign up"` / `"free tool"`) immediately above the fold in Viewport 1.
2. **Prevent Token Dilution:** Strip unnecessary introductory essays above or around the utility; excessive narrative copy risks diluting entity/action focus across the document's opening tokens and triggers pogo-sticking (directional, single-source). Keep supporting instructions compact below the tool.

## 4.2 Organic Link Flywheel & Strict GSC Account Isolation
*(Any DR or backlink velocity outcomes below are directional single-source observations, not guarantees.)*
1. **Verify Crawlability Before Scaling Page Count:**
   - Confirm initial indexation and crawl health in Google Search Console on the first batch (`~70` pages) before expanding directory size (`→ 139+` pages) — never dump thousands of unverified pages simultaneously (`../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.2).
2. **Editorial Internal Linking Across Owned Properties:**
   - Connect genuinely distinct, disclosed niche utilities only where editorial relevance serves readers (never construct hidden PBN rings or manipulate anchor text — comply strictly with `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.3 and `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` FTC common-ownership disclosure rules).
3. **Separate GSC Properties Under Real Verified Owners:**
   - Following [`kirby-seo-telemetry`](../../kirby-seo-telemetry/SKILL.md) Module 9 §9.8 item 5, keep distinct client or independent-project domains in separate Google Search Console properties verified under their real owner/operator accounts for ownership/attribution hygiene and blast-radius separation (comply strictly with Google's policies; never create separate accounts to conceal common ownership).
