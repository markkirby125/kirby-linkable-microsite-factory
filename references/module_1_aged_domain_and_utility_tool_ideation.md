## 1.1 Two High-Yield Asset Archetypes
1. **The Clean-History Encyclopedia / Resource Rebuild (`cheese.help` Model):**
   - Acquire memorable niche/utility domains (`$4–$10` promo registrations or Wayback-vetted clean histories per `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` Module 7 §7.10 C — reject prior pharma/casino/adult pivots).
   - Iterate out of ugly early AI drafts (Mike Hawley bought `cheese.help` new for `$4`, archived his own v1 site in the Wayback Machine, and rebuilt it as a structured cheese encyclopedia ranking Page 1 for **`"mild cheese"`**, earning `3,000–4,200` LLM citations, `25` pages getting traffic, and **`11 MSN` links across `7` pages** with `$0` link-building spend — single-source, directional).
2. **The Exact-Match Free-Utility Microsite (`audiototext.com` / `SEO-TV.com` Model):**
   - Build single-purpose free utilities or niche directories on memorable domains (`audiototext.com` free audio/text tool growing `~50 → ~8,000` clicks/day even through `-50%` temporary dips; `SEO-TV.com` retro video-store directory built in `4–5 hours` indexing `~70 → 139` live pages — single-source, directional).

## 1.2 Keyword Narrowing & Cannibalization Splitting
1. **Cluster & Verify SERP Intent Before Building:**
   - Filter massive keyword sets down to verified, non-overlapping intent targets (e.g., `SEO-TV.com` narrowed `21,180` initial search terms down to `213` curated keywords after inspecting live SERP intent, including **verifying Google actually displays video results on the SERP before building a video page**).
2. **Split Distinct Verticals to Prevent Self-Cannibalization:**
   - Never collapse overlapping modifiers onto one ambiguous page template. Create distinct page types with clear intent jobs (e.g., `SEO-TV.com` launched `Learn SEO` first and held `Business of SEO` and `Local SEO` for Round 2 internal linking).
3. **Interactive Utility Selection:**
   - Prioritize tools users bookmark and journalists cite: interactive maps (e.g., single-source GTA VI Leonida map case), directories with unique Easter eggs/randomizers (`SEO-TV.com` `"randomize me"` button + episode counter), or friction-free zero-signup converters (`audiototext.com`).
